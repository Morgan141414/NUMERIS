## Notes on identity and use

- `INCLUDED` means the source is eligible for the comparison matrix; it does not mean NUMERIS must reproduce every result.
- `EXTENDED` means relevant context is recorded but not used as a direct nearest-neighbour baseline without further extraction.
- The Tiemann et al. paper is included because it directly motivates the inference-distortion test protocol; it is not evidence that any particular controller is superior.
- Official software pages are included as capability records, not as peer-reviewed performance evidence.
- The ledger intentionally avoids treating search snippets, blogs, or vendor summaries as primary evidence.
- `VERIFIED-PRIMARY-INDEX` is used where the direct publisher/official page was inaccessible in the verification pass but the DOI or an authoritative primary index confirmed the bibliographic identity and extracted abstract-level claim. It is not equivalent to a full-text audit.
