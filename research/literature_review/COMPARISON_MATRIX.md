## Interpretation rules

1. A method is not considered distinct merely because it has a new name, software wrapper, or weighted score.
2. “Downstream inference aware” is not a sufficient novelty criterion: DALTON and prior probabilistic/Bayesian methods already operate at or near the likelihood/inference layer.
3. A valid NUMERIS claim would require a formal mechanism, a declared applicability class, and evidence under predeclared equal-budget conditions.
4. If the final controller is mathematically equivalent to an existing adjoint, probabilistic, or likelihood-aware strategy, the project must report that result and reframe the contribution.

## Required evidence before any research-gap statement is upgraded

- source-level derivation of the final estimator and decision rule;
- nearest-neighbour equivalence analysis against Cao–Petzold, Lang–Verwer, probabilistic ODE solvers, and DALTON;
- reproduced inference-distortion benchmark;
- equal-budget comparisons across declared model/noise/budget factors;
- estimator calibration against independent reference errors;
- ablations separating estimator, allocation, and stopping effects;
- explicit negative results and failure regimes.
